Who We Are

A Warm Welcome,
Every Single Time

Mabida Guest House was born from a simple belief: that every traveller deserves to feel at home, no matter how far from their own front door. Located just 1 km from the heart of Butha-Buthe town, we offer modern en-suite rooms with genuine Basotho warmth.

Whether you are in the district for business, visiting family, attending government affairs, or simply passing through on your way through Lesotho — our doors are open and our team is ready to make your stay exceptional.

We take pride in clean, well-kept rooms, reliable 24/7 solar hot water, freshly prepared meals, and a staff that genuinely cares. That is the Mabida promise.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Four principles guide everything we do at Mabida — from how we prepare your room to how we greet you at the door.

Basotho hospitality is not a policy — it is who we are. Every guest is welcomed as family from the moment they arrive.

Our Journey

How Mabida
Came to Be

Mabida Guest House grew from a family vision — to create a place in Butha-Buthe where travellers could experience real comfort and authentic local hospitality.

Starting with a handful of rooms and a lot of heart, we have grown steadily while keeping the same personal, caring touch that made our first guests feel truly at home. Today, we serve business travellers, families, and visitors from across Lesotho and beyond.

The Beginning

A Family Vision

Mabida Guest House opens its doors in Butha-Buthe with a commitment to warm, reliable accommodation for the district's visitors.

Growing Up

Solar Upgrade

We install rooftop solar geysers across all rooms, providing 24/7 hot water independent of Eskom load-shedding schedules.

Expansion

Rooms & Dining

Our en-suite room range expands to include the Family Suite and Deluxe options, alongside our on-site dining room for guests.

Today

Growing with Pride

Rated ★4.8 by our guests, Mabida continues to welcome travellers from Lesotho and beyond with the same warmth we started with.
